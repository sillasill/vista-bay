Vista Bay is a 3D open-world crime game that runs offline in a desktop browser: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted level with police who chase and ram you, four chained story missions from the fixer Marla Voss, pistol and fist combat, a HUD with a live minimap, synthesized sound, and a save that remembers your cash and mission progress. You play Rico Santana, a wheelman fresh out of the Harbor lockup.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move, mouse to look, E to enter or exit a car, click to shoot, F to punch, P to pause.
